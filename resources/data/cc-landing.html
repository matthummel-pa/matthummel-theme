Questions

Before you buy

Is Cobble & Candle a WordPress restaurant theme?

Yes. It is a WordPress block theme for restaurants, taverns, and inns, with menus, live opening hours, table bookings, events, private dining, and rooms to stay, built without a page builder.

Can I buy Cobble & Candle?

Yes. Pick a plan for 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites and check out on this page. Each plan is a yearly license that includes updates and email support. If it has a bug or problem I can't resolve, you get a refund within 30 days.

Does it handle rooms for a B&B or inn?

Yes. Rooms have nightly and weekend prices, a minimum stay, and an availability calendar. Guests send a booking request, you confirm it from the dashboard, and the guest gets an email. Calendars sync both ways with Airbnb, Booking.com, and Vrbo.

Does it have a blog and a newsletter?

Yes. The Journal is a full blog with categories, search, press and diner write-ups, and sign-up forms that send subscribers to Mailchimp, Brevo, MailerLite, Kit, Buttondown or a list on your own site.

Does it take payments?

No, on purpose. Table and room bookings are requests you confirm, so there is no card handling or refund process to manage. Link your online-ordering system for takeout.

Will my hours be right for Google and AI search?

Each location publishes Restaurant structured data with opening and holiday hours, address, map coordinates, cuisine and price range. It works alongside Yoast SEO or Rank Math. The same hours feed your llms.txt file and the assistant tools, so they never disagree.

Can I import my menu?

Yes. Food & drink → Import / export takes a CSV from Excel or Google Sheets, shows a preview of what will be added or updated, and never deletes anything.

Is it accessible?

It is built and tested for WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard paths, screen-reader labels, contrast in all four styles, 44 px touch targets and reduced motion. It also supports right-to-left languages.

What do I need to run it?

WordPress 6.6 or newer and PHP 8.3 or newer. Caching plugins such as LiteSpeed Cache and WP Rocket are supported.

Is Cobble & Candle an AI theme?

It is built for AI search, not by AI and not powered by an AI service. It does not call a model or write your content. It publishes your hours, menus, rooms and events in forms AI search and assistants can read: structured data, a JSON menu feed, an llms.txt file, robots.txt rules for AI crawlers, and read-only tools for assistants.

Which AI services does it work with?

The crawler rules name the published crawlers of OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, Google, Apple, Meta and others. Structured data and llms.txt are open formats any service can read; whether a given service uses them is up to that service. The assistant tools use WordPress’s Abilities API and the Model Context Protocol, so an assistant that supports MCP connectors can use them once the MCP Adapter plugin is installed. Nothing here promises a ranking or a citation.

What do I need for the assistant tools?

WordPress 6.9 or newer, and WordPress’s MCP Adapter plugin to connect an assistant. On older WordPress the tools are simply not there and the rest of the theme, including llms.txt and the crawler rules, works as normal. The tools are read-only and answer only from what your public pages already show: nothing can be booked or changed.

How is this better than a standard WordPress theme?

A standard restaurant theme leaves it to you: hours and menus as text, images or a PDF; an llms.txt you would write and keep current by hand; robots.txt edits for each AI crawler; and no way for an assistant to ask for tonight’s hours or whether a room is free. Cobble & Candle keeps all of that in step with the pages you already edit.
